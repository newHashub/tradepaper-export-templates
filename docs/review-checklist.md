# Document review checklist

Use this alongside the [blank templates](../README.md#choose-a-template). Confirm the fields needed for your actual shipment with the buyer and the people arranging transport and clearance.

## References and parties

- [ ] Invoice and order references are consistent across the documents.
- [ ] Exporter, buyer and consignee names and addresses are current.
- [ ] Invoice date and shipment references are correct.
- [ ] All bracketed placeholders have been replaced or removed appropriately.

## Goods and value

- [ ] Descriptions identify the actual products and match the packing list.
- [ ] Quantities and units are explicit and consistent.
- [ ] Each line amount equals quantity multiplied by unit price.
- [ ] Subtotal, additional charges, adjustments and invoice total reconcile.
- [ ] Currency, payment terms and agreed trade term / named place are explicit.
- [ ] Origin and any required product classification have been verified for the actual goods.

## Cartons and weight

- [ ] Every physical carton is counted once in the carton total.
- [ ] Carton numbering matches the labels and the packing list.
- [ ] Product quantities in the cartons add up to invoice quantities.
- [ ] Net weight describes the goods; gross weight includes the packaging.
- [ ] Gross weight is at least net weight for every carton.
- [ ] Dimensions use the outer packaging and state the measurement unit.
- [ ] Different carton sizes are calculated separately and then summed.
- [ ] Shared cartons are not counted again for each product inside them.

## Final review

- [ ] The buyer's and carrier's requested fields and marks are included.
- [ ] Any shipment-specific declarations or signatures have been completed.
- [ ] The final documents are readable when exported or printed.
- [ ] Real customer or shipment data has not been posted to this public repository.
